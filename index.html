Our Story

For 25 years, G Boyz Wire Wheels has built custom wire wheels for the lowrider community right here in Bell Gardens, California. We didn't grow through ads or flashy marketing - we grew one car show, one cruise, and one word-of-mouth referral at a time. Our reputation was built by riders who trusted us with their rides and told their friends. That same commitment to quality and craftsmanship is what keeps customers coming back generation after generation.
